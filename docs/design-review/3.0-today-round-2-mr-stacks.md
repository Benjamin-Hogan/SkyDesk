## Must-fix

1. **The header entry collides with degraded status.**
   - *Problem:* in `r2-weather-degraded`, `104 overhead ›` runs straight through `Updated 47 min ago`. R1-15 reserves the right side, but nothing stops the left side from growing into it. This is the exact state you're asked to mock (M3).
   - *Fix:* measure first. When a status is showing, the entry drops to `104 ›`; if that still doesn't clear the status by 12 px, hide the text but keep the target. Add a pure `headerEntryFit()` and host-test it at 0, 104 and `1.2k` against the longest status. Re-render `weather-degraded` with a 3-digit count.
2. **`212+` points the wrong way. That's my error from round 1.**
   - *Problem:* losing the set makes re-seen planes count **twice**, so the true count is **at most** 212. `212+` says "at least". In fsb18 the `+` is also a bold glyph that reads as part of the number.
   - *Fix:* `~212` in MUTED fsb18 (the `~` means "don't trust the last digit" here, not linear-counting noise), with glcd DIM `since reboot` under it, the way `learning` has `day 2 of 3`. Update the 11 table and the `today-no-sd-rebooted` render.

## Should-fix

1. **Outage hours erase real passes.** `hourly()` draws the dotted line *instead of* the bar. `today-offline` loses the 3 PM bar that `today-busy` has, so the chart no longer adds up to `31`. Draw the bar, and put the outage mark under it. The 1 px dotted line is also hard to tell from the HAIR baseline at 2 m. Use a 3 px DIM checker stipple under the bar slot.
2. **Chip → map when the plane is gone.** At 250 kt a climbing A350 leaves the 12 nm poll within about 3 min, so for most of the 10-min window, "focused on the passed plane" won't happen.
   - Specify it: the focus order becomes selected > will-pop > just popped (M7, 30 s) > **chip-passed** > nearest.
   - Chip-passed is entered only by a chip tap. It uses M7's tag (`passed · 6.1 mi NE`), with M4's pointer if it's off the map.
   - If the hex has left the traffic, the map opens on its normal focus; say so. Mock `map-from-passed` and `map-from-passed-gone`.
   - This stays consistent with the reserved will-pop chip row: that row wins the chip and the focus.
3. **Device check 1 contradicts M1.** It still says the chip "opens Today with that pass on top". Rewrite it: the chip opens the map on the plane, the header opens Today with it on top.
4. **`today-unsynced` is unreachable.** 11 says the entry is hidden before sync. Either keep the header target live (with no text, per Must-fix 1) or drop the render. I'd keep it: someone can then learn why the page is empty.
5. **After midnight, the rows lie about the day.** `11:58 PM` under `FRI SEP 25` reads as tonight. When any row is from an earlier day, draw its time in DIM and label the section `LAST PASSES · SINCE YESTERDAY`, or put `yday` in glcd DIM in place of `PM`.
6. **Separate the entry from the date.** `SEP 24   31 overhead` reads as a run of numbers; `JUL 31   31 overhead` in the zones render proves it. Put a `drawSep()` dot between them. It doesn't compete with the hero: f2 MUTED against fsb24 is fine at 2 m.
7. **Host tests the spec still lacks** (my round-2 checklist): the list surviving midnight, age and window from close, rarest `none new`, the rarest tie-break, and junk-code exclusion. The response says there's a midnight test; neither `test/host` nor 11 → Tests has it.
