  apart from the inlined state.
- Spec: `docs/12-setup-portal.md`.
- Host tests: `test/host/test_setup.cpp`. All pass, including a module-for-module QR match

**On phone images:** headless Edge stopped writing screenshots on this machine partway through

## Must-fix
1. **Cancel: fixed.**
   - It is now `Hold to cancel`, 112 × 36, fires on a 1 s hold (`SETUP_HOLD_MS`), and fills
     while held (`r2-portal-cancel-hold`).
   - The boot button is 220 × 36 (`r2-boot-error-portal`).
2. **SAMPLE on the device: fixed.**
   - The firmware inlines `var S={…}` at `/*STATE*/`, so there's no second request.
   - SAMPLE is used only for `file:` or `127.0.0.1`; the device is always 192.168.4.1.
   - With no state, the page shows `Can't read SkyDesk's settings` with a Reload button, and Save
     never appears.
3. **Smart punctuation: fixed.**
   - Both parsers treat any symbol as a separator.
   - A host test uses the owner's string typed with `’ ”`.
   - The page's `#far` state is typed that way too.
   - Hint under the field: `Decimal, or like 33 21 10 N 111 47 20 W`.
4. **The gate: two gates.**
   - Streets use `SETUP_GATE_MI` (0.1 mi). Radar uses `SETUP_RADAR_GATE_MI` (1 mi, about 2
     radar pixels; the clutter mask has 1-pixel cells).
   - 0.4 mi now reads `Reads as … · 0.4 mi from Gilbert, AZ. Too far for the street map, which will
     show planes without streets. Radar and weather work.` (the `#near` state).
   - Distances under 10 mi get one decimal. Host-tested.
5. **Empty scan: fixed.**
   - `netChange()` runs in `build()`, so the name box shows at once.
   - `Scan again` (44 pt) reloads `/?scan=1`.
   - The note reads `No networks found. SkyDesk sees 2.4 GHz networks only.`
   - Found in passing: with no saved SSID, the password box said "Unchanged". It now says
     "Password".

## Should-fix
1. **Joined vs page open:** `r2-portal-joined-nopage` (`No page? Open Safari to 192.168.4.1`)
   until `GET /` is served, then `r2-portal-page-open` (`Finish on your phone`).
2. **Saved wording:** now `If it can't join, tap Set up from phone on SkyDesk.`
3. **Reason codes:**
   - `setupReasonVisible` and `setupReasonWords` drive both the auto-entry and the boot hint:
     `Wrong password?` (`r2-boot-error-portal`) or `Not found - 2.4 GHz only?`
     (`r2-boot-error-notfound`).
   - Auto-entry also applies after a mid-run drop.
4. **Auto cycle:**
   - 5 min idle after an automatic entry (`SETUP_AUTO_IDLE_S`), and a touch extends it.
   - An abandoned joined session ends at 30 min (`SETUP_SESSION_S`).
5. **Password:**
   - 8 lowercase letters without i/l/o (one keyboard page), generated once per device and kept in
     NVS (`apPw`).
   - Host-tested: the alphabet never needs QR escaping.
6. **Page a11y:**
   - Show and Scan are 44 pt; Reset is 44 pt (a link-button).
   - The password field has right padding.
   - `role="alert"` on the error, which now sits **above** Save.
   - The failing field is outlined, scrolled to and focused.
   - The time zone has a label.
   - `aria-pressed` on the compass buttons.
7. **Stale name and time-zone hint:**
   - Beyond the radar gate, a Name still equal to the build place is cleared.
   - `Looks like Eastern.` is suggested from the longitude.
8. **Wording:** `Reads as`; `center`.
9. **Off-gate labels:**
   - The map reads `No streets here - built for Gilbert, AZ`.
   - The radar adds `Weather and planes use your location.`
10. **Backlight:** the portal forces full backlight (spec → Modes 4).
11. **Long SSIDs:** the auto title and the Saved line fall back to `your WiFi`
    (`r2-portal-auto-long-ssid`, `r2-portal-saved-long-ssid`).
