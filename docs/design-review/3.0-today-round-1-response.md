     it's still in the traffic. This extends M7's after-pop focus, and it never outranks a
     will-pop plane.
   - The strip opens the live card with its route. The council's frozen-card replay is withdrawn.
2. **The route drops on the rows that need it: fixed.**

   - Routes are shown only if `routePlausible` passes; otherwise the registration, else the
     callsign.
3. **Entry points: accepted.**
   - The clock entry is dropped.
   - The header row, y 0–36 full width, is the Today entry, with `31 overhead ›` after the date
     (`r2-weather-header-entry-0` / `-104`, `r2-weather`, `r2-weather-degraded`,
     `r2-weather-header-entry-night`).
   - The hero band moves to y 36–112. `r2-weather-rain-cue-zones` overlays the three tap zones: the
     cue sits 20+ px inside the radar zone.
4. **Today must say when it wasn't watching: accepted.**

   - An hour with more than 5 min of outage draws a DIM dotted baseline.

     (`r2-today-unsynced`).
   - With no SD after a reboot, the count reads `212+` MUTED (`r2-today-no-sd-rebooted`). I chose
     `+` over keeping the set in NVS: no extra flash wear, and it's honest.
5. **Midnight: accepted.** LAST PASSES survives midnight; the counts and bars reset
