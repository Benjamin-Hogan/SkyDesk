# 3.0 Today's Sky, round 1: Mr Stacks

Read: AGENTS, 06, 07, the council record, 11, `today_screen.py`, the `screens.py` hand-off, 08/09/10 touch tables. Judged on every `r1-*@3x.png` against `final-weather-rain-cue`, `final-map-inbound`.

## Score: A 6/10 · B 6/10

**A (chip).** Visually it's right: one line, TEXT/MUTED split, amber only on the icon, clean fallbacks. The behaviour sinks it: the chip silently changes destination on a 10-minute timer, and that takes the map away exactly when the sky is busy.

**B (Today).** Worth a screen. Three big numbers, a shape of the day, the last three passes. The hierarchy reads from 2 m. But the rows drop the route on the very pass that deviation 1 relies on, and the page can't tell you when it wasn't watching.

## Must-fix

1. **The passed chip steals the map.**
   - *Problem:* the chip is the only way from weather to the map (08). The chevron looks the same in both states, but in the passed state the tap goes to Today. At PHX (the worst mock has 12 passes an hour), the chip sits in the passed state for hours, so the map is unreachable. The tap's meaning changes on a timer the user can't see.
   - *Fix:* one target, one meaning. The chip opens the **map** in every state. In the passed state the map focuses the passed hex while it's still in traffic, which extends M7's 30 s focus to the passed window, and only when no plane is will-pop. The strip then opens the live card, with the route: that's "replay" without a frozen mode. Today gets its own entry (M3). If you keep chip → Today, mock how the map is reached in ≤ 2 taps during a bank.
   - *Rule:* 08 navigation; predictable targets.

2. **The route drops on the rows that need it.**
   - *Problem:* `British Airways A350-1000` loses `PHX → LHR` in both busy and worst. The long-haul, the one pass worth asking about, loses its route. The time column (`12:59 PM`, 58 px) is what costs it.
   - *Fix:* draw row times as `7:21` in f2 MUTED, with a glcd DIM `PM` beside it like the clock. That gives about 44 px, and `BA A350-1000 + PHX → LHR` fits at about 297/310. Keep identity-beats-route for anything longer. Show a route only if it passes `route_plausible`; there's no room for `UNVERIFIED`, so an implausible route falls back to the registration.
   - *Rule:* R1-3, deviation 1.

3. **Entry points: a collision, a target that's too small, and nothing to find.**
   - *Problem:* the clock block ends at y 92, and the rain cue (`Rain 10 mi W ›`) starts at about y 93, directly below it. There's zero gutter between Today and radar, and a 56 px fingertip straddles both. The header target (y < 26) is under 36 px. None of the three entries shows an affordance, except a chip that exists 10 min after a pass.
   - *Fix:* drop the clock entry; the clock is read, not tapped, and it's been radar since v3. Make the header row (y 0–36, full width) the entry, and move the hero band to y 36–112 (the hero content starts at about 38). After the date, draw `31 overhead ›` in f2 MUTED on the **left**. The right side stays reserved (R1-15). That teaches the page and gives a glance stat. Mock it at 0, 31 and 104, and in the degraded header.
   - *Rule:* touch ≥ 36 px; R1-15; v3-R1-2 (the cue's slot).

4. **Today must say when it wasn't watching.**
   - *Problem:* an hour with Traffic offline draws as a zero bar, which says "quiet", like radar's "No rain" lie. There's no Today state for Traffic offline or for an unsynced clock. Without SD, the nearby hash set (on SD only) is lost on reboot, so re-seen planes are counted twice on top of the NVS count.
   - *Fix:* when Traffic is offline, the header right shows `Traffic offline` WARN, which outranks no-SD. Hours with more than 5 min of outage draw a DIM dotted baseline, not a zero. Before sync, show `Waiting for clock` MUTED in the list slot. Without the set, show the nearby count as `212+` MUTED, or keep the 512 B set in NVS, and measure the wear.
   - *Rule:* "never lies" (v3-R1-1), R2-3.

5. **Midnight.**
   - *Problem:* a pass at 11:58 PM, then at 12:03 AM the chip reads `Passed 5 min ago` and Today is empty. Device check 1 fails.
   - *Fix:* LAST PASSES isn't reset at midnight (it's "last", not "today's"). The counts and bars reset. The empty message shows only when the list is empty. Add a host test.

## Should-fix

1. **The chart needs a title.** The peak `6` sits over `6P` and reads as "6 PM". Add `PASSES BY HOUR` in glcd DIM at the panel's top-left. Hour labels go to MUTED; at night, DIM glcd vanishes (night render). Mark the current hour with a TEXT tick even at zero (the empty day has no "now").
2. **The right column needs one colour.** Routes are TEXT and registrations are MUTED. Make both MUTED (the arrow already is) with a gap of at least 12 px. In worst, `ERJ-175 DFW` runs together.
3. **Passed age from close, not peak.** For a hovering helicopter or a circling trainer, the peak can come minutes before EXIT. The chip would say `Passed 8 min ago` a minute after the card left, then expire. Count both the age and the 10-min window from close. The list keeps the peak time.
4. **Flicker.** The page draws straight to the TFT and "redraws when the version changes", while nearby changes every 5 s poll. Redraw only the cells that changed; the bars and list change on pass events only.
5. **No SD:** the spec (a glcd line) and the mock (the header) disagree; pick the header. `--` reads as broken; use `needs SD` in fs9 MUTED.

## Deviations
1. **No card replay: accept.** A frozen card that the tracker can confuse with a live pop is a worse bug than a missing feature. That's conditional on M1 (the live card via the map) and M2 (the route actually shows).
2. **Entry points: reject as drawn.** See M3: one visible header entry, no clock entry.
3. **Rarest = fewest lifetime sightings: accept.** Specify:
   - the state when nothing qualifies (`none new`, MUTED)
   - the tie-break (prefer a type that passed overhead, then the most recent)
   - the exclusion of junk type codes (blank, `TWR`, ground vehicles)
4. **No SD in the header slot: accept.** It's genuinely degraded, since radar needs the card too, and R1-15 is the right slot. Resolve S5.

**Privacy:** passes. Labels come via `planeLabels()`, and the owner is excluded from the CSV and tested.
**Amber:** clean; only the chip icon.
