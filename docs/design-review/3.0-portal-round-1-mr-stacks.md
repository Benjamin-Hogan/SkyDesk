# 3.0 Setup portal, round 1: Mr Stacks

Read: AGENTS, 07, the council record, 12, `portal_screen.py`, the `offgate` path in `map_screen.py`, `portal/index.html` (HTML and JS). Judged on `r1-{settings-menu, portal-*, boot-error-portal, map-offgate, radar-offgate}@3x`, compared with `final-boot-error`, `final-setup-facing` and `final-weather`, and on `portal/r1-phone-states.png` (sample network renamed to `HomeNet`; device renders re-rendered with it, otherwise unchanged).

## Score: A 6/10 · B 6/10

**A (device).** The QR is right. It's 132 px (about 24 mm) with a full 4-module white quiet zone, dark on light, and an iPhone reads that from 15–30 cm. The password in fsb9 is the loudest text on the screen, as it should be. The four steps match what iOS actually does. The screen falls down on its one touch target, and on the button that leads to it.

**B (phone).** The page is calm, has one column and 17 px inputs (so no zoom), and says honestly what a far location costs. But on the device it can silently show made-up data, and an iPhone's own keyboard breaks the DMS input the spec promises.

## Must-fix

1. **Cancel is 30 px and reboots on one tap.**
   - *Problem:* it's the only target on the portal screen, and people will pick up a 90 g CYD to aim the camera at it. A thumb at the bottom left throws away the session. The boot button is 34 px, and the spec promises ≥ 36.
   - *Fix:* make Cancel ≥ 36 px tall and fire it on a **1 s hold** with a fill, like the "settings on hold" rule. Once a phone is joined, show `Hold to cancel`. Make the boot button 36 px (y 200–236).
   - *Rule:* touch ≥ 36 px; R2-9.

2. **The page falls back to SAMPLE on the device.**
   - *Problem:* `fetch('/state')…catch(()=>SAMPLE)`. If `/state` fails once (and the sync WebServer is busy with probes), the page shows the sample `HomeNet`, Gilbert and `hasPass:true`. Save would then post that network with a blank password as "keep".
   - *Fix:* have the firmware inline the state into the served page (`var S={…}`), so there's no second request. Use SAMPLE only when `location.protocol=='file:'`. If the state is missing, show `Couldn't read SkyDesk's settings` and a Reload button, and disable Save.
   - *Rule:* never lie; "served byte-for-byte" means the mock code ships.

3. **iOS smart punctuation breaks DMS.**
   - *Problem:* the iPhone keyboard turns `'` and `"` into `’` and `”`, and `autocorrect="off"` doesn't reliably stop it. `parseLoc` strips `′″` but not `‘’“”`, so the owner's own string fails when typed on the phone.
   - *Fix:* strip U+2018/2019/201C/201D (and `º`) as well. Add the host test `33°21’10”N 111°47’20”W`. Put a hint under the field: `or 33 21 10 N 111 47 20 W` (no symbols needed; the parser already takes it).

4. **The gate lies at small distances.**
   - *Problem:* 0.3 mi from the build centre prints `0 mi from Gilbert, AZ … the radar is off`. A neighbour two streets away loses both radar and streets over what is less than a pixel of radar.
   - *Fix:* use two gates. Streets keep `SETUP_GATE_MI`. The radar gate is the radar tile's own tolerance (it's generated, so compute it in `make_basemap.py`). Print distances under 10 mi with one decimal.
   - *Rule:* honest previews; v3-R1-1 spirit.

5. **An empty scan hides the name box.**
   - *Problem:* if there are no networks and no saved SSID, `net.value=''` (Other…), but `otherBox` is only shown in `onchange`, so there's nowhere to type an SSID. The scan also ran before the phone joined, and there's no way to re-scan.
   - *Fix:* call the `onchange` logic in `build()`. Add `Scan again` (a 44 pt link that reloads `/state?scan=1`). When the list is empty, show `No networks found. SkyDesk sees 2.4 GHz only.`

## Should-fix

1. **Joined is not the same as the page being open.** The joined state drops the steps, but the brief's hard case is a phone that joined and got no sheet. The firmware knows when `GET /` is served. Use `Phone joined · no page? Open Safari to 192.168.4.1` until that happens, then `Finish on your phone`.
2. **The Saved promise is false.** `comes back … in 3 minutes` is only true if the network is visible. A mistyped SSID under Other… is never visible. Say: `If it can't join, tap Set up from phone on SkyDesk.`
3. **Name the failure by reason code.** Decide "failing" from the disconnect reason: AUTH_FAIL or HANDSHAKE_TIMEOUT means visible and failing; NO_AP_FOUND means not visible. The boot hint then says `Wrong password?` or `Not found · 2.4 GHz only` truthfully. The latter was in the old hint and was dropped.
4. **The auto cycle is 83 % portal.** A router that's up but refusing clients recovers up to 15 min late. After an automatic entry, idle-exit at 5 min, and let a touch extend it. Also cap an abandoned joined session at 30 min since the last request.
5. **Password typing.** Specify the alphabet in 12. Uppercase plus digits means Shift on every letter and toggles to `123` on iOS. Consider lowercase-only letters with no `l` or `o` (24⁸, fine for a 15-min AP). Also consider one password per device kept in NVS: iOS remembers the network, and a new password each session makes a hand-typed join fail with "Incorrect password".
6. **Page targets and a11y.**
   - Show (34 px) and Reset (36 px) go to ≥ 44.
   - The password input gets `padding-right:64px` so text doesn't run under Show.
   - `#err` gets `role="alert"`.
   - The time zone select needs a `<label>`.
   - On an error, scroll to and outline the field. The error currently appears below Save, and only the first one shows.
7. **Stale name.** When the location moves outside the gate and Name still equals the build place, clear it. Suggest a time zone from the longitude (`Looks like Eastern`).
8. **Wording.** `Saved as` before anything is saved should be `Reads as`. The owner is in the US, so use `center`.
9. **The off-gate map label** `Map is for Gilbert, AZ` reads as "this is Gilbert". Use `No streets here · built for Gilbert, AZ`. The radar screen is honest and clear. Add `Weather and planes use your location.`
10. **The portal forces full backlight** whatever the night-dim setting. A dimmed QR scans worse.
11. **A long SSID in the auto title.** A 32-char SSID in fsb9 overflows. Fit it or fall back to `Can't join your WiFi`. Do the same on the Saved screen.

## Notes on C
- **Auto-entry:** visible + 3 min and never when invisible is the right principle. Pin down "failing" (S3), and say whether it also applies after a mid-run drop. It should.
- **Clear-the-flag-first:** good. Also write the settings as one NVS commit *before* the Saved screen shows.
- **Privacy:** passes. `/state` carries `hasPass` only. Add: the `[http]` log must never print the POST body.
- **QR escaping:** our SSID and alphabet contain none of `\ ; , : "`, so no escaping is needed today. Write the escaper anyway, host-test it, and assert the alphabet excludes those characters, so renaming the AP later can't break the QR. Fixed version 4 throws above 62 bytes, so test the longest SSID.
- **Settings with 4 rows:** each row is 36 px, which passes, but the 4 px gutters are tight on resistive touch. Hitting Phone setup by mistake costs two reboots. Acceptable if it fires on release.
